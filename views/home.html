Welcome to CineVerse

CineVerse is a web-based system for booking movie tickets and managing cinema hall operations. Customers can browse movies and book seats online, while staff, food staff and administrators manage shows, food orders and the whole system.

Login

Our Services

  • Online Ticket Booking - Browse movies, choose a show and seat, and book tickets online.
  • Food and Beverage Ordering - Order popcorn, drinks, snacks and combo packs for your seat.
  • Hall and Show Management - Staff manage halls, show times and seat availability.
  • System Administration - Admins manage users, movies and reports.
